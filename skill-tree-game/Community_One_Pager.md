# Building Future Web Developers: A Student-Driven Learning Platform

## **The Class Experience**
Over the past semester, high school students participated in an innovative web development course using a custom-built "Skill Tree Game" – a Civilization-inspired learning platform that transforms coding education into an engaging, gamified experience.

**What Made This Different:**
- Students chose their own learning paths through interconnected skills
- Real client projects with community mentors (Jeremy, Fiona, and Jonathan)
- AI-powered coaching and peer collaboration tools
- Daily reflections tracking both technical and personal growth

---

## **Student Growth Highlights**

### **Technical Mastery**
Students progressed through 3 skill tiers, from foundational HTML/CSS to advanced problem-solving:
- **Strong completion rates** on Tier 1 foundations (Cultural Mapping, Code Implementation, Client Discovery)
- Students built **interactive projects** including tic-tac-toe games with AI opponents and gaming-themed websites
- Advanced students reached **Tier 2 skills** including **Advanced CSS**, **Image Generation**, **User Testing**, and **Peer Feedback**
- Real client work included pet-sitting websites and custom visual content creation

### **Soft Skills Development**
Student reflections on their skill evidence revealed remarkable personal growth:
- **"It help me become more creative and also make coding more interesting for me and make thing easier to do"** - Charles, on Cultural Asset Mapping
- **"The more question I ask, the more information I get"** - Charles, discovering the value of client communication
- **"I learn that not every website ur building do not require the same thing and we should be specific about what we want"** - Charles, on advanced CSS and project specificity
- Students self-rated competencies in **STEAM Interest**, **Sense of Belonging**, and **Communication**
- Team-based client projects developed **collaboration** and **project management** skills

### **Real-World Impact**
- **6 student teams** created functional websites for community clients
- Students learned **Scrum methodology** with sprint boards and defined roles
- **Sprint retrospectives** showed students taking ownership of their learning journey

## **Platform Evolution: Responding to Student Voice**

Since August 2025, we've made **15+ major improvements** based on direct student feedback:

### **Enhanced Communication** *(Student Request: "We need better ways to collaborate")*
- Built **team messaging system** with image sharing and code formatting
- Added **group conversations** for client teams
- Implemented **URL hyperlinking** for resource sharing

### **Streamlined Reflection Process** *(Student Request: "Daily questions feel repetitive")*
- **Dynamic reflection prompts** tailored to each day's activities
- **Image upload capability** for sprint boards and website screenshots
- **Self-assessment tools** for metacognitive skill development

### **Improved User Experience** *(Student Feedback: "Sometimes the app feels clunky")*
- **Real-time notifications** for messages and achievements
- **XP leaderboard** to celebrate top performers
- **Website gallery** for students to showcase their work

### **Teacher Dashboard Enhancements** *(Teacher Request: "I need better visibility into student progress")*
- **Competency analytics** powered by AI analysis of student work
- **Visual reflection viewer** with image support
- **Real-time progress tracking** across all skill areas

## **The Technology Behind Learning**

**Built by Students, for Students:** This platform was developed using the same technologies students learn:
- **React** frontend with **Firebase** backend
- **AI integration** (Claude 4) for personalized coaching
- **Real-time collaboration** tools
- **Mobile-responsive design** for anywhere learning

**Data-Driven Insights:** The platform captures rich learning analytics:
- **Skill progression patterns** showing individual learning paths
- **Peer interaction data** revealing collaboration effectiveness
- **Reflection sentiment analysis** tracking emotional engagement
- **Time-to-mastery metrics** for curriculum optimization
